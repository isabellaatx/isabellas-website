Isabella Banse

My name is Isabella Banse; I am a sophomore at the University of Colorado Boulder, currently pursuing a B.S. in Computer Science. I am from Austin, Texas, and am involved the Chi Omega fraternity, the Society of Women Engineers, and Partnerships for Informal Science Education in the Community (PISEC). I am currently very interested in machine learning, specifically neural networks, and just completed a project where I created a neural network to classify different genres of music based on synthetic EEG readings. I would like to learn more about the science and code behind features and circuits, as well as the process behind how a system rewrites itself if one feature is changed. My personal next step is to complete Andrej Karpathy's Neural Networks: Zero to Hero Youtube course to further my knowledge. I'd also like to develop a creative volume slider site soon that reads the octave that a user is singing/speaking into the computer and adjusts the volume accordingly.
